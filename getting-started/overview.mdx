# Get started with Bites

Open [Bites](https://bites.com), sign in, and select the workspace you intend to
operate. A workspace is the primary boundary for business records, members,
permissions, integrations, and Vision sites.

## The platform model

1. **Account** identifies the person signing in.
2. **Workspace** contains one business context and its data.
3. **Modules** enable product capabilities for that workspace.
4. **Roles and permissions** decide what each member can view or change.
5. **Sites and integrations** connect physical locations and external systems.

## First-login checklist

1. Confirm the workspace name in the workspace switcher.
2. Ask the workspace owner to enable the modules you need.
3. Confirm that your role includes the required view or manage permission.
4. Follow the relevant product guide from the navigation.
5. For API access, create or obtain an approved bearer token and send the active
   workspace with each workspace-scoped request.

<Warning>
  Never paste API tokens, camera credentials, migration credentials, or one-time
  site tokens into screenshots, tickets, or public documentation.
</Warning>
