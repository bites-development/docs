# Identity Verification Privacy and Retention

Bites stores only the local workflow data needed for auditability:

- local verification IDs
- Stripe verification session/report IDs
- encrypted verified output summaries
- status and error codes
- reviewer decisions
- audit events

Raw ID images are not stored in Bites. The identity provider processes the
documents under its own retention controls.

For an authorized deletion request, contact the workspace owner or organization
support channel. The request can include provider redaction and removal of local
encrypted outputs while preserving the minimum non-sensitive audit evidence
required by policy or law.
